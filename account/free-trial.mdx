AiSky gives you 7 days and 100 AI voice minutes to experience the full product at no cost and with no commitment. There is no setup fee, no credit card required to start, and no watered-down feature set — you get the same AI receptionist your paying customers use, from the very first call.

## What's Included

Your free trial gives you complete access to the AiSky platform:

- **100 AI voice minutes** to use across the 7-day period
- **Full product access** — every feature available on the monthly plan
- **All 11 official South African languages** for inbound calls
- **Calendar integration** to test real appointment booking flows
- **Live dashboard** with call logs, messages, and usage tracking
- **1 AI agent** configured for your business during onboarding

Nothing is locked or restricted during the trial. You are testing the real product.

## How to Start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the AiSky website">
    Go to [aisky.co.za](https://www.aisky.co.za) and click **Start Free Trial**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Enter your business name, email address, and a password. No credit card details are required at this stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your AI agent">
    Follow the onboarding steps to set your business hours, preferred language, and call handling rules. Your AI receptionist will be ready to take calls within minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start receiving calls">
    You will be assigned a South African business number. Share it, forward your existing number to it, or test it directly — your 7-day trial begins from the moment your account is created.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  There is **no setup fee** and no credit card required to start your free trial. You only need to add a payment method when you choose to upgrade to the monthly plan.
</Note>

## Trial Limits

Your free trial includes **100 AI voice minutes**. Once those 100 minutes are consumed, the trial ends and your AI receptionist stops answering new calls — regardless of whether you are still within the 7-day window. The trial also ends automatically after 7 days even if you have minutes remaining.

Plan your testing accordingly. Use the live dashboard to track how many minutes you have used at any point during the trial.

## Upgrading

You can upgrade to the monthly plan (R1,999/month) at any time during your trial — you do not need to wait for it to expire:

1. Go to **Settings → Billing** in your dashboard.
2. Add your card details.
3. Confirm your subscription.

Your service continues without interruption and your existing agent configuration, call history, and settings are preserved. Your billing cycle starts from the date you upgrade.

<Tip>
  Make the most of your trial by testing with **real inbound calls** — forward your existing business number to your AiSky number and let the AI handle live callers. This gives you a far more accurate sense of how AiSky performs than test calls alone.
</Tip>

## After the Trial

If you do not upgrade before your trial ends, your AiSky service **pauses** — the AI receptionist stops answering calls, but your account and all its data remain intact. Your configuration, call logs, messages, and contact records are retained for **30 days** after the trial expires.

If you decide to upgrade within that 30-day window, your data is restored and your agent picks up exactly where it left off. After 30 days, your data is permanently deleted.
