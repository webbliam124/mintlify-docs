AiSky keeps pricing simple and transparent — one flat monthly plan with no setup fees, no lock-in contracts, and no surprises. Everything your business needs to run a 24/7 AI voice receptionist is included from day one.

## Monthly Plan

The AiSky monthly plan is **R1,999/month**, charged on the same date each month from when you sign up. Your plan includes:

- **250 AI voice minutes** per month
- **5 simultaneous call slots** — handle up to 5 callers at the same time
- **1 dedicated South African business number**
- **1 AI agent** configured for your business
- **Live dashboard** to monitor calls, messages, and usage in real time
- **Calendar integration** for appointment booking
- **All 11 official South African languages** supported

## Additional Minutes

When your 250 included minutes are used up, AiSky continues answering your calls without interruption. Additional minutes are billed at **R0.97 per minute**, using true per-second billing — you only pay for the time actually used.

**Example:** A 20-second call costs approximately **R0.32** (20 ÷ 60 × R0.97).

This means short calls — a quick message, a number enquiry, a brief transfer — cost a fraction of a full minute. You are never rounded up to the nearest minute.

## No Hidden Fees

AiSky charges one price for one plan. There are no extras to worry about:

- **No setup fee** — your account is ready the moment you sign up
- **No contract** — your subscription runs month to month
- **Cancel any time** — no penalties, no notice period required

## Billing

Your subscription renews automatically each month on your original sign-up date. Payment is by card and is processed securely at the start of each billing cycle. Any overage minutes used during the previous month are included on your next invoice as a single line item.

If your card payment fails, AiSky will retry and notify you by email. Your service remains active during the retry window.

<Note>
  You can monitor your real-time minute usage at any time from the **Usage** section of your AiSky dashboard. Usage figures update after each completed call.
</Note>

<Tip>
  AiSky automatically sends you email alerts when you reach **80%** and **100%** of your included 250 minutes, so you are never caught off guard. Learn how these alerts work in [Usage Alerts](/account/usage-alerts).
</Tip>
