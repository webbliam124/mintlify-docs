AiSky automatically monitors your monthly minute consumption and sends you email alerts as you approach and reach your included 250 minutes. You do not need to configure anything — the alerts fire on their own, so you always know where you stand before your bill arrives.

## Automatic Alerts

AiSky sends two automatic email alerts every billing cycle:

- **80% alert** — sent when you have used 200 of your 250 included minutes. You still have around 50 minutes remaining, giving you time to review usage or prepare for potential overage.
- **100% alert** — sent when your 250 included minutes are fully consumed. From this point, any further calls are billed at R0.97/min on a per-second basis.

No setup is required. Both alerts are active on every account from the moment you sign up, and they reset automatically at the start of each new billing cycle.

<Note>
  Alert emails are sent to the **account owner email address** registered on your AiSky account. If you need alerts to reach a different address, update your account email under **Settings → Account Details**.
</Note>

## Viewing Current Usage

You can check your live usage at any point during the month without waiting for an alert:

1. Log in to your AiSky dashboard.
2. Navigate to **Usage** in the left-hand menu.
3. View your current minutes used, minutes remaining, and a breakdown by day.

The dashboard updates after each completed call, so the figures are always current.

## What Happens at 100%

When your included minutes run out, **AiSky does not pause or stop answering calls**. Your AI receptionist continues to operate without interruption. Calls beyond your 250 minutes are simply billed as overage at R0.97/min (true per-second billing — a 20-second call costs approximately R0.32).

Your overage total accumulates through the rest of the billing cycle and appears as a single line item on your next monthly invoice. There is no service interruption, no approval required, and no action needed on your part.

## Managing Spend

If you want to keep close tabs on your usage and avoid unexpected overages, here are a few practical steps:

- **Review your call log regularly.** Long calls — for example, complex appointment bookings — consume more minutes. Identifying these helps you understand your usage patterns.
- **Check the dashboard after busy periods.** If your business has seasonal peaks or a promotional campaign running, check your usage more frequently during those windows.
- **Set a personal reminder.** Around the 15th of each month, log in and confirm you are on track. If you have already used more than half your minutes in the first half of the month, plan for possible overage.
- **Consider call flow optimisation.** If you consistently exceed 250 minutes, review your AI agent's call handling. Shorter, more efficient flows reduce average call duration.

<Tip>
  Make it a habit to check your usage dashboard once a week — it only takes a few seconds and helps you spot unusual call volumes early before they affect your bill.
</Tip>
