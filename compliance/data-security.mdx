AiSky takes the security of your call data seriously. Every call your AI receptionist handles may contain sensitive personal information — caller names, contact details, appointment times, and business messages. That data deserves rigorous protection at every stage, from the moment a call connects to the moment you view it in your dashboard.

## Encryption in Transit

All communication between callers, the AiSky platform, and your dashboard is encrypted using **TLS (Transport Layer Security)**. This includes:

- The voice connection during every inbound call
- API communications between AiSky and any integrations you connect (such as your calendar)
- Your browser session when you log in to the dashboard

No call audio or transcript data travels over an unencrypted connection.

## Encryption at Rest

Once a call ends, all associated data is written to secure, encrypted storage. This includes:

- **Call recordings** (where enabled)
- **Call transcripts**
- **Caller details** captured during the call
- **Messages** taken on your behalf
- **Appointment records** created through the AI agent

Data at rest is protected using industry-standard encryption. Encryption keys are managed securely and are not accessible to unauthorised parties.

## Access Control

Your call data belongs to your business. Access is restricted accordingly:

- Only **users you have authorised** on your AiSky account can view your call logs, transcripts, and messages.
- **AiSky staff do not access your call content** in the normal course of operations. The only exception is when you contact support and explicitly grant consent for a support agent to review specific records in order to resolve an issue.
- Access logs are maintained so that any interaction with your data can be audited if required.

To manage who has access to your account, go to **Settings → Team** in your dashboard.

## Account Security

You play an important role in keeping your account secure:

- **Use a strong, unique password** for your AiSky account. Avoid reusing passwords from other services.
- **Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)** from **Settings → Security**. This adds a second verification step at login and significantly reduces the risk of unauthorised access.
- **Do not share your login credentials** with anyone outside your team. Add additional users through the team settings instead.
- **Log out** of the dashboard when using shared or public devices.

If you believe your account has been compromised, change your password immediately and contact AiSky support.

## Incident Response

AiSky monitors its infrastructure for unusual activity and security anomalies. In the event of a confirmed security incident affecting your data, AiSky will notify you as required under applicable law and work with you to contain and remediate the issue.

If you discover or suspect a security vulnerability or incident involving AiSky, report it immediately by emailing [hello@aisky.co.za](mailto:hello@aisky.co.za). Include as much detail as possible — what you observed, when, and any affected records or features. All reports are treated with urgency and in strict confidence.
