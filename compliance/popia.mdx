The Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) is South Africa's primary data privacy law. It governs how personal information is collected, stored, used, and shared. If your business uses AiSky to answer calls, your callers are sharing personal information — their name, phone number, reason for calling, and any details they provide to the AI receptionist. Understanding how POPIA applies to that data protects both your callers and your business.

## What POPIA Means for Your Business

Under POPIA, there are two key roles when personal information is processed:

- **Responsible party** — the entity that determines the purpose and means of processing personal information. In most cases, **your business is the responsible party**. You decide why calls are being answered and what happens with the information collected.
- **Operator** — an entity that processes personal information on behalf of the responsible party. **AiSky acts as your operator (data processor)**. AiSky processes caller data on your instructions and for your business purposes.

As the responsible party, your business holds the primary obligation under POPIA to ensure that personal information is handled lawfully, with purpose, and with appropriate security. AiSky supports you in meeting those obligations through its data handling practices, but your accountability as the responsible party remains.

## How AiSky Handles Caller Data

When a caller speaks to your AiSky receptionist, the following data may be collected and stored:

- Caller phone number
- Caller name (if provided during the call)
- Call transcript
- Messages taken on your behalf
- Appointment details (if the caller books a slot)

All of this data is stored securely in your AiSky account. It is **encrypted in transit** using TLS and **encrypted at rest** using industry-standard encryption. Only your authorised account users can access your call data. AiSky staff do not access call content unless you explicitly request support and grant consent to do so.

See [Data Security](/compliance/data-security) for full details on encryption and access controls.

## Data Retention

AiSky retains your call data for as long as your account remains active. If your account is closed, data is retained for **30 days** before permanent deletion. You can request deletion of specific records at any time through the dashboard or by contacting [hello@aisky.co.za](mailto:hello@aisky.co.za).

If one of your callers submits a deletion request to your business under POPIA, you are responsible for honouring that request. AiSky will assist you in locating and deleting the relevant records upon your instruction.

## Consent and Disclosure

POPIA and general good practice require that individuals are aware their information is being collected. AiSky can be configured to play a **disclosure message** at the start of every inbound call — for example:

> *"Thank you for calling. This call is handled by an AI receptionist and may be recorded for quality and record-keeping purposes."*

Enabling this message is recommended. You can customise the exact wording in your AI agent settings under **Settings → Call Handling → Intro Message**.

## Your Obligations

As the responsible party under POPIA, you are expected to:

- **Keep your AiSky account secure.** Use a strong password and enable two-factor authentication. Do not share your login credentials.
- **Only share call data as permitted under POPIA.** Do not export or distribute caller transcripts and messages to third parties without a lawful basis.
- **Honour deletion requests.** If a caller asks you to delete their personal information, act on the request promptly and instruct AiSky to delete the associated records.
- **Appoint an information officer** if required under POPIA for your business size and sector.
- **Review your AI agent's disclosure message** to ensure callers are appropriately informed before sharing personal information.
