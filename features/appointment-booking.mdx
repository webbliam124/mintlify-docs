When a caller wants to book an appointment, AiSky handles the entire process without any involvement from you. It checks your live calendar availability, offers suitable time slots, confirms the booking with the caller on the spot, and sends a notification to your business — all within a single phone call. Your calendar stays accurate, your caller leaves satisfied, and you never have to play phone tag to schedule a meeting.

<Note>
  Appointment booking requires a connected calendar integration. Set up your calendar before enabling this feature. See [Calendar Integration](/setup/calendar-integration) for step-by-step instructions.
</Note>

## How It Works

The caller experience is smooth and conversational from start to finish.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Caller requests an appointment">
    The caller indicates they would like to book — whether they say "I'd like to make an appointment," "Can I schedule a visit?" or anything similar, AiSky recognises the intent and moves into booking mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AiSky gathers the details">
    AiSky asks for any information needed to find the right slot — such as the type of appointment, preferred date or time, or which staff member the caller would like to see (if configured).
  </Step>
  <Step title="AiSky checks live availability">
    AiSky queries your connected calendar in real time and identifies available slots that match your configured booking rules — including business hours, appointment duration, and buffer times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A slot is offered and confirmed">
    AiSky offers the caller one or more available options. Once the caller selects a slot, AiSky confirms the booking verbally and immediately creates the calendar entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirmations are sent">
    The caller receives a verbal summary of their booking before the call ends. Your business receives an instant notification via your preferred channel — email, SMS, or dashboard alert.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Customising Your Booking Settings

You have full control over how AiSky manages your appointment availability. Configure the following in your **AiSky Dashboard** under **Appointment Booking → Settings**:

- **Appointment duration** — set the default length for each booking type (e.g., 30 minutes for a consultation, 60 minutes for a full assessment)
- **Buffer time** — add time between appointments so you are never rushed from one to the next
- **Available hours** — define which days and times AiSky is permitted to book, independently of your general business hours
- **Blackout dates** — block specific dates such as staff leave, public holidays, or office closures so no bookings are accepted on those days
- **Custom booking questions** — collect additional information during the booking process, such as the nature of the appointment or a preferred staff member

Each of these settings can be adjusted at any time and takes effect immediately on your next inbound call.

## Confirmation and Notifications

Once a booking is made, both the caller and your business are kept in the loop:

- **Caller** — AiSky confirms the date, time, and any relevant details verbally before ending the call
- **Business owner** — you receive a notification through your chosen channel (email, SMS, or dashboard) with the caller's name, number, appointment type, and confirmed time slot

All bookings appear in your **AiSky Dashboard** under **Call Log**, where you can view, edit, or cancel them alongside the full call transcript.
