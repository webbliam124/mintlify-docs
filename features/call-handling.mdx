Whether you run a medical practice, a plumbing company, or a boutique, AiSky ensures every caller reaches a responsive, helpful voice — not a ringing phone.

## Natural Conversation

AiSky does not rely on simple silence detection to know when a caller has finished speaking. Instead, it monitors seven real-time conversation signals to identify the natural end of a caller's turn:

- **Prosodic cues** — changes in pitch and rhythm that signal the end of a thought
- **Syntactic closure** — detecting when a sentence is grammatically complete
- **Pragmatic completion** — recognising when the caller's communicative intent has been fulfilled
- **Breath pattern** — natural breathing pauses that indicate a handoff
- **Energy decay** — trailing off in vocal energy at the end of an utterance
- **Discourse markers** — words and phrases like "so yeah" or "that's it" that signal a turn end
- **Turn-yielding cues** — explicit or implicit invitations for the other party to respond

The result is a conversation that feels natural and unhurried — AiSky never cuts you off mid-sentence, and never leaves an awkward silence waiting for you to speak again.

AiSky also uses **emotion mirroring**, drawing on 57 distinct emotion styles to match the tone of the caller. A frustrated caller is met with calm reassurance; an enthusiastic caller is met with warmth and energy. This makes every interaction feel genuinely human.

## Caller Qualification

Before taking any action, AiSky gathers the information your business needs. By default, it captures:

- **Caller name** — confirmed at the start of the call
- **Caller number** — logged automatically from the inbound call
- **Reason for calling** — understood through natural conversation
- **Custom fields** — any additional information you configure in your dashboard, such as appointment type, preferred location, or account number

You define what matters to your business. AiSky asks the right questions, listens carefully, and records the answers accurately — every time.

## Call Transfer

When a call requires a human touch, AiSky hands it over seamlessly. You configure the transfer rules in your dashboard to match your workflow. Common examples include:

- Transfer immediately if the caller says "urgent" or "emergency"
- Transfer to a specific number during business hours
- Transfer after qualifying questions have been answered
- Transfer if the caller explicitly asks to speak to a person

<Note>
  Set up and manage your transfer rules directly in the **AiSky Dashboard** under **Call Handling → Transfer Rules**. You can create multiple rules, prioritise them, and assign different destination numbers for different scenarios.
</Note>

Transfers can be configured as warm (AiSky introduces the caller before connecting) or cold (AiSky connects directly). If no one answers the transfer destination, AiSky returns to the caller and offers to take a message instead — so the caller is never simply dropped.
