South Africa is one of the most linguistically diverse countries in the world, and your customers reflect that. AiSky is built to serve them all. It supports all 11 official spoken languages of South Africa, automatically detecting the language your caller uses from the very first sentence and responding naturally in kind — with no configuration needed on your part. Whether your caller opens in isiZulu, Afrikaans, or Sesotho, AiSky keeps up.

## Supported Languages

AiSky handles inbound calls in all of the following official South African languages:

| Language | Also Known As |
|---|---|
| South African English | — |
| Afrikaans | — |
| isiZulu | Zulu |
| isiXhosa | Xhosa |
| Sesotho | Southern Sotho |
| Setswana | Tswana |
| Sepedi | Northern Sotho / Sesotho sa Leboa |
| Tshivenda | Venda |
| Xitsonga | Tsonga |
| siSwati | Swati |
| isiNdebele | Southern Ndebele |

Beyond language alone, AiSky recognises South African place names, suburbs, regional references, and local business terminology — so your callers can speak naturally without having to spell things out or adapt to a generic international voice assistant.

## Automatic Language Detection

AiSky identifies the language your caller is speaking in real time. There is nothing to press, no language menu to navigate, and no option to "press 2 for Zulu." AiSky simply listens and responds in the same language — immediately and naturally.

This works whether a caller speaks a single language throughout the call or code-switches (mixing languages mid-sentence, which is common in South African conversation). AiSky is built for the way South Africans actually speak.

## Setting a Primary Language

While AiSky detects language automatically, you can set a **primary language** for your account. This is the language AiSky defaults to when it initiates the call greeting before the caller has spoken.

To set your primary language:

1. Log in to your **AiSky Dashboard**.
2. Navigate to **Settings → Voice & Language**.
3. Select your preferred **Primary Language** from the dropdown.
4. Click **Save Changes**.

Your greeting and any outbound prompts (such as appointment confirmations) will be delivered in your primary language. Inbound detection and response remain automatic regardless of this setting.

<Tip>
  Keep your business knowledge base — including your services, FAQs, and staff names — written in the language or languages your customers most commonly use. AiSky draws on this content when answering questions, so the more naturally it is written, the more fluently AiSky will communicate it to callers.
</Tip>
