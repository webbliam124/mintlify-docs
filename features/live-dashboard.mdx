The AiSky Dashboard is your central view into everything happening with your business calls. From the moment a call comes in to the moment it ends, every detail is recorded, organised, and made available to you in real time. You can see what callers said, how calls were resolved, how many minutes you have used this month, and what is happening on active calls right now — all without leaving a single screen.

## Call Log

Every inbound call handled by AiSky is recorded in your Call Log. Each entry includes:

- **Timestamp** — the date and time the call was received
- **Duration** — total call length in minutes and seconds
- **Caller ID** — the caller's number (where available)
- **Outcome** — how the call was resolved, displayed as one of the following:

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Booked | AiSky successfully scheduled an appointment |
| Message | AiSky took a message from the caller |
| Transferred | The call was handed over to a human |
| Missed | The call came in but could not be answered (e.g., all 5 slots occupied) |

Use filters to sort your Call Log by date range, outcome type, or caller ID. Click any entry to open the full call transcript.

## Call Transcripts

Every call that AiSky handles produces a full, verbatim transcript. You can read exactly what was said — by both AiSky and the caller — at any point after the call ends.

Transcripts are useful for:

- Reviewing the accuracy of information captured during qualification
- Confirming what was promised or communicated to a caller
- Identifying common questions or concerns you may want to address in your knowledge base
- Resolving any disputed call details

Transcripts are searchable. Use the search bar within the **Transcripts** view to find calls containing specific words or phrases.

## Real-Time Monitoring

The **Live Calls** view shows you every call currently in progress. For each active call you can see:

- The caller's number (where available)
- How long the call has been running
- The current stage of the conversation (greeting, qualification, booking, transfer, etc.)

This view refreshes automatically — there is no need to reload the page. Use it to stay aware of call volume during busy periods or to monitor a specific call in real time.

## Usage Stats

The **Usage & Billing** section gives you a live snapshot of your consumption for the current billing period:

- **Minutes used** — total AI voice minutes consumed so far this month
- **Minutes remaining** — how many of your included 250 minutes are left
- **Per-minute rate** — a reminder that additional minutes are billed at R0.97/min on true per-second billing
- **Alert status** — whether any usage threshold alerts you have configured are active

You can set usage alerts in **Settings → Billing Alerts** to receive an email or SMS notification when you reach a defined percentage of your included minutes — for example, at 80% and again at 100%.

## Exporting Data

Download your call data for use in your own reporting, CRM, or compliance records.

To export your Call Log:

1. Navigate to **Call Log** in the Dashboard.
2. Apply any filters you want (date range, outcome type, etc.).
3. Click **Export** in the top-right corner.
4. Select **CSV** as your format and click **Download**.

The exported file includes all visible columns: timestamp, duration, caller ID, outcome, and a link to the transcript.
