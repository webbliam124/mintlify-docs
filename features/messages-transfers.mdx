Not every call ends with a booking, and not every call should stay with AiSky. Sometimes a caller simply needs to leave a message; other times, the situation calls for a human. AiSky handles both gracefully. It captures complete, accurate messages and delivers them to you immediately, and it transfers calls to the right person at the right moment — based on rules you configure. Between these two features, no caller is ever left without a resolution.

## Message Taking

When a caller cannot be helped by booking an appointment or when no transfer destination is available, AiSky takes a message. The process is natural and conversational — callers are not reciting to a voicemail prompt, they are speaking to an attentive AI that listens, clarifies, and records.

AiSky captures the following for every message:

- **Caller name** — confirmed during the conversation
- **Caller number** — logged automatically from the inbound call
- **Message content** — recorded verbatim and summarised for clarity
- **Timestamp** — the date and time the call was received

### Message Delivery

Once the call ends, your message is delivered through every channel you have configured:

- **Email** — sent immediately to your nominated address
- **SMS** — a brief summary sent to your business number
- **Dashboard** — full message available in your **AiSky Dashboard** under **Messages**

<Tip>
  Set a **custom message template** in your dashboard under **Messages → Template Settings**. You can define exactly how messages are formatted when delivered — for example, leading with the caller's number, grouping by urgency keyword, or adding a custom subject line to email notifications. This makes it easy to triage messages at a glance.
</Tip>

## Call Transfers

When a call needs a human, AiSky makes the handover smoothly. You define the rules; AiSky handles the execution.

### Configuring Transfer Rules

Set up your transfer rules in the **AiSky Dashboard** under **Call Handling → Transfer Rules**. You can create as many rules as your business requires. Common examples include:

- Transfer immediately if the caller uses keywords such as "urgent," "emergency," or "complaint"
- Transfer after AiSky has completed qualifying questions
- Transfer if the caller explicitly asks to speak to a person
- Transfer based on the time of day or day of the week
- Transfer to different numbers depending on the department the caller needs

Rules are evaluated in priority order. You can reorder, enable, or disable them at any time.

### Warm vs. Cold Transfers

AiSky supports two transfer modes:

- **Warm transfer** — AiSky introduces the caller to your team member before connecting, providing a brief handover summary so the human knows who they are speaking to and why
- **Cold transfer** — AiSky connects the caller directly without a spoken introduction, for situations where speed is the priority

You can assign a transfer mode per rule, so urgent calls can be connected instantly while routine transfers include a warm handover.

### If No One Answers

If the transfer destination does not answer, AiSky does not simply drop the caller. It returns to the call, informs the caller that the line is unavailable, and offers to take a message instead. The caller always leaves with a resolution.

<Note>
  Minutes spent on transferred calls count against your included monthly minutes. This includes both the time AiSky spends speaking to the caller before the transfer and any hold or introduction time during the handover. Monitor your usage in the **AiSky Dashboard** under **Usage & Billing**.
</Note>
