This page brings together the questions businesses ask most often before and after getting started with AiSky. If you can't find what you're looking for here, reach out to the team at hello@aisky.co.za.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does AiSky work with my existing phone number?">
    Yes. AiSky works with your current business number — you don't need to change it or port anything. You simply set up call forwarding on your existing line so that calls route to AiSky when you're unavailable, busy, or outside business hours. Your number stays exactly as it is.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which carriers are supported?">
    AiSky works with all major South African carriers, including **Telkom**, **Vodacom**, **MTN**, **Cell C**, and **Rain**. If your business uses a SIP trunk or an on-premise PBX system, AiSky supports those too, so you can integrate it into your existing telephony setup without switching providers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when all 5 call slots are in use?">
    AiSky supports up to **5 simultaneous calls**. If all slots are occupied when a new call comes in, the caller's experience depends on your carrier's overflow setting — most callers will hear a busy signal, while some carriers will send the call to voicemail instead. You can review your carrier's overflow configuration if you'd prefer a specific behaviour.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can AiSky book appointments for my business?">
    Yes. AiSky can book appointments on your behalf during a call, provided you have an active calendar integration set up in your dashboard. Once connected, AiSky checks your available slots in real time and books directly into your calendar. You'll receive a notification for each new booking, and so will your caller.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which languages does AiSky support?">
    AiSky supports all **11 official spoken languages of South Africa** — including isiZulu, isiXhosa, Afrikaans, Sepedi, Setswana, Sesotho, Xitsonga, siSwati, Tshivenda, isiNdebele, and English. The language is automatically detected from the caller's speech, so your callers can communicate in the language they're most comfortable with without pressing any options.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does billing work?">
    The standard plan costs **R1,999 per month**, which includes **250 AI voice minutes**. If your business uses more than 250 minutes in a billing cycle, additional minutes are charged at **R0.97 per minute**, billed per second so you're never charged for time you didn't use. Your monthly invoice is generated at the start of each billing period, and you can view a full call log and usage breakdown in your dashboard at any time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a contract or cancellation fee?">
    No. AiSky is a month-to-month subscription with no fixed-term contract and no cancellation fee. You can cancel at any time from your account settings, and your service will remain active until the end of your current billing period. There are no hidden fees or penalties for leaving.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is AiSky compliant with POPIA?">
    Yes. AiSky is fully compliant with the **Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)**. All call data and caller information is encrypted in transit and at rest. AiSky does not share your business data or caller data with third parties, and you retain full control over the data collected through your account. If you have specific compliance questions, contact hello@aisky.co.za.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does setup take?">
    Most businesses are up and running in **about one hour**. Setup involves creating your account, configuring your business profile and knowledge base, connecting your calendar (optional), and enabling call forwarding on your carrier line. No engineering or technical expertise is required — the onboarding flow guides you through each step.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can AiSky transfer a call to a human?">
    Yes. You can configure call transfer rules in your dashboard to hand off calls to a specific person or team based on criteria you define — for example, transferring all calls that mention a specific topic, or all calls received during business hours. AiSky will inform the caller before transferring, and you can set a fallback action if the transfer destination doesn't answer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I try AiSky for free?">
    Yes. AiSky offers a **7-day free trial** that includes **100 AI voice minutes** at no cost. No credit card is required to start the trial. This gives you enough time to configure your receptionist, test it with real calls, and evaluate whether it's the right fit for your business before committing to a paid plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What do I do if AiSky is giving callers wrong information?">
    If AiSky provides incorrect or outdated answers, the first step is to update your **business knowledge base** in the dashboard — this is the source AiSky draws on when responding to caller questions. You can add or edit custom FAQs, update your business details, and re-crawl your website if you've connected it as a knowledge source. If the problem persists after updating your knowledge base, contact the support team at hello@aisky.co.za and they'll help you diagnose the issue.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
