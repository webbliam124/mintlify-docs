Every call your business receives follows a consistent, intelligent process managed entirely by AiSky. When a customer dials your number, AiSky answers instantly, converses naturally in the caller's preferred South African language, collects the information you need, takes action on their behalf, and delivers a structured summary to you — all without interrupting your day. The sections below explain each stage of that process.

## 1. Caller dials your existing number

Your business keeps its current phone number. You activate call forwarding at the carrier level or via your PBX, so every inbound call is routed to AiSky's infrastructure before it ever rings on your desk phone or mobile. From the caller's perspective, they simply dialled your number and someone answered.

## 2. AiSky answers — instantly, every time

AiSky picks up within two rings, 24 hours a day, seven days a week, including public holidays. There is no hold music, no voicemail prompt, and no missed call. The AI opens with the custom greeting you configured in your dashboard, such as *"Good morning, thank you for calling Bright Legal — how can I help you today?"*

<Note>
  AiSky handles up to **5 simultaneous inbound calls** on a standard plan. If your business regularly receives more concurrent calls, contact [hello@aisky.co.za](mailto:hello@aisky.co.za) to discuss a higher-capacity configuration.
</Note>

## 3. The AI detects language and responds naturally

AiSky listens to the caller's opening words and automatically switches to their preferred language. It supports all 11 official spoken South African languages: Zulu, Xhosa, Afrikaans, English, Northern Sotho (Sepedi), Tswana, Sotho, Tsonga, Swati, Venda, and Ndebele. No caller needs to press a number or select a language — the switch happens transparently within the first exchange.

## 4. AiSky qualifies the caller

Rather than simply taking a message, AiSky asks intelligent follow-up questions based on your business profile and knowledge base. For example, a medical practice might have AiSky ask whether the call is urgent, which practitioner the patient needs, and whether they are an existing patient. The qualification criteria are fully configurable in your dashboard under **Call Flows**.

## 5. AiSky takes action

Depending on the caller's needs and the rules you have set, AiSky will take one or more of the following actions:

- **Book an appointment** — AiSky checks your connected calendar (Google Calendar or Microsoft 365) and offers the caller available slots in real time, then confirms the booking and sends a calendar invite.
- **Take a message** — AiSky captures the caller's name, number, and reason for calling in a structured format.
- **Answer a question** — AiSky draws on your knowledge base to respond to common questions about pricing, location, hours, and services.
- **Transfer to a human** — If the caller requests a person, or if AiSky determines the call needs human intervention based on your escalation rules, it performs a warm transfer to the staff member or queue you have designated.

## 6. You receive a call summary

Within seconds of the call ending, AiSky sends a summary to your nominated email address (and optionally via SMS or webhook). Each summary includes the caller's name and number, the language used, a brief transcript of the key points, the action taken, and any follow-up items flagged for your team.

## 7. Review and improve over time

Your dashboard logs every call with a full transcript, sentiment indicator, and action record. Use this data to refine your call flows, update your knowledge base, or identify patterns — such as frequently asked questions your website does not yet answer — so AiSky gets more accurate over time.

<Tip>
  Visit **Dashboard → Insights** each week to review your most common caller intents. Updating your knowledge base in response to real call data is the fastest way to improve AiSky's accuracy.
</Tip>
