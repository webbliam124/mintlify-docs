AiSky is a 24/7 AI voice receptionist built specifically for South African businesses. It answers every inbound call in the caller's preferred language — across all 11 official spoken languages — so you never miss a lead, a booking, or an urgent message. Whether you run a solo practice, a multi-branch operation, or anything in between, AiSky plugs into your existing phone number in about an hour, with no engineering required.

## Up and running in three steps

From sign-up to your first live call, the setup process is straightforward and requires no technical background.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your free account">
    Visit [aisky.co.za](https://aisky.co.za) and click **Start Free Trial**. Enter your business details to provision your dedicated AiSky number. Your 7-day, 100-minute trial begins immediately — no credit card required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up call forwarding">
    Log in to your dashboard, navigate to **Phone Setup**, and follow the carrier-specific instructions to forward your existing business number to AiSky. The whole process takes under five minutes and works with all major South African carriers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Call your business number to confirm AiSky answers correctly, adjust any greeting or language preferences in your dashboard, and then remove the trial flag to start handling real customer calls around the clock.
  </Step>
</Steps>
