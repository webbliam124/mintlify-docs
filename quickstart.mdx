This guide walks you through everything you need to get AiSky answering your business calls. By the end, your existing business number will forward to AiSky, the AI will greet callers in their preferred South African language, and you will receive call summaries straight to your inbox. The entire process takes under an hour and requires no technical expertise.

<Tip>
  AiSky offers a **7-day free trial** with 100 AI voice minutes included — no credit card required. That is enough capacity to fully test the receptionist in a real environment before committing to a paid plan.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start your free trial">
    Open [aisky.co.za](https://aisky.co.za) in your browser and click **Start Free Trial**. Fill in your business name, industry, and contact email address. AiSky will provision a dedicated inbound number for your account and send your login credentials by email within a few seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your business information">
    Log in to your AiSky dashboard and navigate to **Business Profile**. Paste in your website URL — AiSky reads your site to learn about your services, opening hours, pricing, and FAQs so it can answer caller questions accurately from day one. You can also add or override specific details manually in the **Knowledge Base** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up call forwarding">
    Go to **Phone Setup** in your dashboard to find your unique AiSky forwarding number. Then activate call forwarding on your existing business number using the instructions for your carrier:

    | Carrier | Forward all calls | Cancel forwarding |
    |---------|-------------------|-------------------|
    | **Telkom** | Dial `*21*[AiSky number]#` | Dial `##21#` |
    | **Vodacom** | Dial `**21*[AiSky number]#` | Dial `##21#` |
    | **MTN** | Dial `**21*[AiSky number]#` | Dial `##21#` |
    | **Cell C** | Dial `**21*[AiSky number]#` | Dial `##21#` |
    | **Rain** | Configure forwarding in the Rain app under **Voice Settings** | Toggle off in the same menu |

    If your business uses a PBX or SIP trunk, select **SIP / PBX** in the Phone Setup screen and follow the SIP credential instructions instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test your receptionist">
    Call your business number from any mobile or landline. AiSky will answer with your custom greeting, respond to questions about your business, and offer to book an appointment or take a message. Check your dashboard under **Recent Calls** to review the call summary and transcript. Adjust the greeting, language preference, or escalation rules in **Settings** if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    When you are satisfied with how AiSky sounds and responds, open **Account → Trial** in your dashboard and click **Activate Full Plan**. Select the R 1,999/month plan (250 AI voice minutes included, with additional minutes billed at R 0.97/min). Your receptionist will now handle every inbound call, day and night, with no further action required from you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Need help during setup? Email the AiSky support team at [hello@aisky.co.za](mailto:hello@aisky.co.za) and a human will respond within one business day.
</Note>
