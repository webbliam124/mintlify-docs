AiSky learns about your business by reading your website and any additional information you provide — no manual scripting required. Point AiSky at your site URL and it automatically crawls your pages, extracting your services, location, pricing, team details, and frequently asked questions. From there, you can fill in any gaps with custom knowledge, set your business hours, and choose the name and greeting your AI receptionist uses on every call.

<Note>
  The initial website crawl typically completes within **2 to 5 minutes** for most small-business sites (under 100 pages). Larger sites may take up to 15 minutes. You will receive an email notification when the crawl is done and AiSky is ready to answer questions about your content.
</Note>

## Set Up Your Business Knowledge

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter Your Website URL">
    In the AiSky dashboard, go to **Knowledge → Website** and paste your business website URL into the field provided. Click **Start Crawl**.

    AiSky will visit every page it can find on your site — including your homepage, services, about, contact, and FAQ pages — and index the content. You do not need to do anything else during the crawl.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review What AiSky Learned">
    Once the crawl finishes, open **Knowledge → Review**. You will see a list of every page AiSky indexed, along with a plain-English summary of what it extracted from each one.

    Scan through the list and look for:
    - **Missing pages** — if AiSky missed a page, click **Add URL manually** and paste the direct link.
    - **Outdated content** — if your website has stale information, update the site first, then click **Re-crawl** to refresh AiSky's knowledge.
    - **Incorrect extractions** — if AiSky misread a piece of content, you can override it with a custom FAQ (see the next step).

    <Tip>
      Keep your website up to date — it is AiSky's primary source of truth. Whenever you change your prices, services, or team, update your site and trigger a re-crawl from **Knowledge → Website → Re-crawl** so AiSky stays current without any manual edits.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Custom FAQs, Business Hours, and Services">
    Your website may not cover everything a caller might ask. Add supplementary knowledge under **Knowledge → Custom**.

    **Business Hours**
    Set your opening hours for each day of the week. AiSky uses these to tell callers when you are open and to qualify appointment booking requests against your availability.

    **Custom FAQs**
    Click **Add FAQ** to enter a question-and-answer pair. Write the question the way a caller would actually ask it — for example, *"Do you offer payment plans?"* — and provide the complete answer you want AiSky to give. You can add as many FAQs as you need.

    **Services**
    List each service or product you offer, including a short description and price (if applicable). AiSky uses this list to qualify callers and route them to the right person or booking type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Greeting and AI Agent Name">
    Go to **Settings → Agent Identity** to personalise how AiSky introduces itself.

    - **Agent name**: Choose a name for your AI receptionist (e.g. *Lerato*, *Sipho*, or simply *AiSky*). This is the name callers will hear.
    - **Greeting script**: Write the opening line AiSky says when it picks up — for example, *"Good day, thank you for calling Cape Town Dental. I'm Lerato, how can I help you today?"*
    - **Language preference**: Select the default language for new callers. AiSky will switch to any of South Africa's 11 official languages mid-call if the caller speaks in a different language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test with Sample Questions">
    Before going live, use the **Test Agent** panel in the dashboard to ask AiSky questions as if you were a caller. Type or speak a question and review AiSky's response.

    Try questions like:
    - *"What are your hours on Saturday?"*
    - *"How much does a consultation cost?"*
    - *"Where are you located?"*
    - *"Can I book an appointment for next Tuesday?"*

    If any answer is wrong or incomplete, go back to **Knowledge → Custom** and add or correct the relevant FAQ. Repeat until you are satisfied with the responses.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keeping Knowledge Fresh

AiSky does not automatically re-crawl your website — you control when it refreshes. As a best practice, trigger a re-crawl any time you:

- Update your pricing or service list
- Add new team members or change contact details
- Publish new pages that callers might ask about
- Change your business hours for public holidays

You can schedule automatic monthly re-crawls from **Knowledge → Website → Crawl Schedule** if you prefer a hands-off approach.
